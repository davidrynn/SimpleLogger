# SimplerCounter Privacy Policy

_Effective October 19, 2026_

SimplerCounter is made by David Rynn. This policy covers the SimplerCounter apps for
iPhone and Apple Watch.

## The short version

SimplerCounter doesn't collect any data. The developer can't see your logs.

## What the app stores, and where

The logs you make, their entries, times and notes are stored:

- **On your devices**, in the app's own storage.
- **In your iCloud account**, if you're signed in to iCloud, so your iPhone and Apple
  Watch stay in sync. They're kept in your private iCloud database, which only you
  can access. Apple stores this data under the
  [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What the app doesn't do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking, and no advertising.
- No third-party code or services.
- No access to your location, contacts, photos, health data or anything outside the app.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a log in the app deletes it and its entries on all of your devices. Deleting
the app removes the data stored on that device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to
**Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage**, choose SimplerCounter,
and delete its data.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new
effective date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/davidrynn/SimpleLogger/issues).
