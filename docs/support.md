# SimplerLogger Support

## How it works

- **Make a counter** for each thing you keep track of, such as "Water plants". Under
  **Each tap**, choose **Starts or stops a timer** if you want to time it, like "Reading".
- **Tap a counter's button** on the list to count it now. For a timer, tap once to start
  and again to stop.
- **Open a counter** to see its history by day, change times, add notes, or delete entries.
- **On Apple Watch**, tap a counter to count it or to start or stop its timer. Counters
  are made and edited on iPhone.

## Common questions

**My watch doesn't show my counters.**
Syncing between iPhone and Apple Watch uses iCloud. Check that both are signed in to
the same Apple Account, that iCloud is turned on for SimplerLogger (**Settings →
[your name] → iCloud**, under apps using iCloud), and that both have a network
connection. Changes can take a minute to arrive.

**Can I rename or reorder a counter?**
Yes. Swipe right on a counter, or tap **Edit** and then the counter, to rename it. In
**Edit**, drag the handles to reorder.

**How do I delete something?**
Swipe left on a counter or an entry. The app asks before deleting. Deleting a counter
deletes all of its entries.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
