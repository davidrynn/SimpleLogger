# SimplerLogger Support

## How it works

- **Make a log** for each thing you track, such as "Water plants". Choose **Timer** if
  you want to time it, like "Reading".
- **Tap a log's button** on the list to record it now. For a timer, tap once to start
  and again to stop.
- **Open a log** to see its history by day, change times, add notes, or delete entries.
- **On Apple Watch**, tap a log to record it or to start or stop its timer. Logs are
  made and edited on iPhone.

## Common questions

**My watch doesn't show my logs.**
Syncing between iPhone and Apple Watch uses iCloud. Check that both are signed in to
the same Apple Account, that iCloud is turned on for SimplerLogger (**Settings →
[your name] → iCloud**, under apps using iCloud), and that both have a network
connection. Changes can take a minute to arrive.

**Can I rename or reorder a log?**
Yes. Swipe right on a log, or tap **Edit** and then the log, to rename it. In
**Edit**, drag the handles to reorder.

**How do I delete something?**
Swipe left on a log or an entry. The app asks before deleting. Deleting a log
deletes all of its entries.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
